## What Is Lead Qualification (And Why WhatsApp Changes Everything)?

Lead qualification is the process of determining whether a prospect is a good fit for your product or service before passing them to your sales team.

Traditionally, this involves:

1. Capturing the lead's contact information
2. Asking qualifying questions (budget, authority, need, timeline — BANT)
3. Scoring the lead based on responses
4. Routing qualified leads to the right salesperson
5. Following up on unqualified leads at the right time

**The problem?** This entire process is manual, slow, and inconsistent.

**WhatsApp changes everything** because:

- **98% open rate** — your messages get seen
- **2B+ active users** — your prospects are already on WhatsApp
- **Conversational format** — leads naturally share information in chat
- **24/7 availability** — chatbots never sleep

When you combine WhatsApp's reach with AI chatbot intelligence, you get a **lead qualification machine** that works around the clock.

## How AI Chatbots Automate Lead Qualification

An AI-powered WhatsApp chatbot doesn't just answer questions. It:

### Captures Leads 24/7
Your chatbot greets every incoming message, captures the prospect's name, phone number, and key details — even while your team is asleep.

### Asks Intelligent Qualifying Questions
Instead of a static form, the chatbot holds a natural conversation. It asks BANT questions naturally:

- *"What size is your business?"*
- *"Which service are you looking for?"*
- *"What's your budget range?"*
- *"When are you looking to start?"*

### Scores Leads in Real-Time
Based on responses, the chatbot assigns a lead score (Hot/Warm/Cold) and decides the next action:

- **Hot lead** → Book a demo immediately → Notify sales team via WhatsApp
- **Warm lead** → Send brochure + schedule follow-up in 24 hours
- **Cold lead** → Add to nurture sequence

### Integrates with Your CRM
Every qualified lead is automatically pushed to your CRM (Supabase, HubSpot, Salesforce, Google Sheets — whatever you use).

### Learns from Every Conversation
The AI remembers past conversations. If a lead returns after 3 weeks, the chatbot picks up where it left off — no repeating questions.
