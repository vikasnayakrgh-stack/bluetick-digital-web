# 04. Content Strategy — Bluetick Digital

## 1. Brand Voice & Tone
* **Authoritative & Engineering-Grade:** Direct, precise, technical, zero fluff.
* **Outcome-Led Business Framing:** Focus on lead qualification speed (<30s), conversion recovery, and reduced manual overhead.
* **Swiss Precision:** Clear typography, sharp section numbering (`01 /` through `06 /`), structured list layouts.

---

## 2. Core Messaging Hierarchy

```
Primary Headline (Hero):
"Websites + AI that turn your digital presence into a growth engine."

Subheadline:
"Build a high-performance website, automate repetitive business processes, and create a digital system that helps your team move faster."

Pillar 01 (Websites & E-commerce):
"High-converting business & e-commerce websites starting from ₹10,000. Built for speed, conversions, and seamless scalability."

Pillar 02 (AI & WhatsApp Automation):
"Custom AI agents and WhatsApp workflows that qualify leads 24/7, sync with your CRM, and eliminate manual follow-up."
```

---

## 3. Strict Pricing & Portfolio Copy Guidelines

### Pricing Copy Standard:
- Websites & E-commerce MUST always be framed with `"Starting from"`:
  - Business Website: *from ₹10,000*
  - Premium Profile Website: *from ₹20,000*
  - Small E-commerce: *from ₹30,000*
  - Large Catalog E-commerce: *from ₹50,000*
- Mandatory Disclaimer Box:
  > *"Final pricing depends on scope, features, integrations, design requirements, catalog size and business requirements."*
- AI Automation: Framed as custom workflow pricing tailored to operational requirements.

### Portfolio / Showcase Copy Standard:
- Showcase cards MUST display: `"Concept / Demo System"`.
- Never invent client names, logo graphics, or fake metrics.
