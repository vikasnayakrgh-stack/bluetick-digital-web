# 01. Project Brief — Bluetick Digital

## 1. Executive Overview
**Bluetick Digital** is an AI-powered Digital Transformation Partner for growing businesses, e-commerce brands, manufacturers, startups, and local service providers across India. 

The primary business value proposition is bridging the gap between custom web development and AI business process automation:
> **"Websites + AI that turn your digital presence into a growth engine."**

---

## 2. Business Context & Market Positioning
* **Primary Offerings:** 
  1. High-Performance Web & E-commerce Infrastructure (Vite/React, mobile-first, Core Web Vitals optimized).
  2. Custom AI Automation Workflows (WhatsApp AI sales agents, automated lead qualification, CRM/Supabase sync, n8n orchestrations).
* **Target Audience:**
  - Indian E-commerce Brands (D2C)
  - Industrial Machinery & B2B Manufacturers
  - SaaS & Tech Startups
  - High-ticket Service Providers (Real Estate, Healthcare, Coaching)
* **Positioning:**
  - Swiss Architectural Style: Engineering precision, editorial typography (`Plus Jakarta Sans`), asymmetric structural grids, hairline dividers (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`).
  - Business-First Technology Partner (NOT a generic web design template agency).
